New Outlook is ready for most of your workflows - delegation depth is what to check first.

Microsoft's feature matrix for New Outlook was last updated June 5, 2026. The app is generally available, continues to receive feature updates, and is the direction of travel for Outlook on Windows.

01 / HEADLINE
GA

Generally available

New Outlook moved out of preview in August 2024 and is supported for eligible Microsoft 365 customers.

EA

Calendar delegation is available

New Outlook supports assigning calendar delegate access, private-event visibility, category management, and delegate routing choices.

AI

Copilot meeting flows are stronger

You get thread summaries, meeting agenda drafting, meeting prep, and recap access wherever your licensing and permissions allow.

!

How to think about it: treat New Outlook as your daily driver for scheduling, email triage, meeting prep, and Copilot-assisted work, and keep a short checklist of the advanced classic Outlook workflows you still depend on.

What's new since you last looked at New Outlook.

This skips the familiar "classic versus new" basics and focuses on the recent movement that changes how you support your executive.

02 / WHAT'S NEW
UpdateGA July 2026Delegation

Events can now be scheduled from a shared or delegate mailbox

Message Center post MC1326502 (Roadmap 561912) lets you organize events on behalf of your leader from a shared or delegated mailbox, so invitations, updates, and notifications send from that mailbox identity. Targeted release ran through June 2026, GA across July 2026.

  • What this means for you: the headline change in this refresh. Microsoft names executive assistants and chiefs of staff directly - your invites finally carry the recognized organizational identity instead of your personal mailbox.
  • Before you rely on it: enabled by default and governed by your existing Exchange permissions. Check your delegate and shared mailbox access before you count on it.
UpdateCopilotGA Aug 2026

Copilot moves into the compose window

Message Center post MC1430542 (Roadmap 564605) puts Copilot side-by-side in the Outlook compose experience so a draft can be created, refined, and edited in real time. Rolling out mid-July to early August 2026 on Outlook web, New Outlook, and mobile; Mac follows, classic Outlook later in 2026.

  • What this means for you: this is the core drafting loop for executive correspondence - iterate in place instead of round-tripping between a Copilot pane and the message body.
  • Before you rely on it: requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot license. Worth demonstrating live against a real reply-on-behalf-of scenario.
UpdateJuly 2026Calendar

Calendar power-user gaps are starting to close

Ctrl+drag now copies a calendar event, and dragging an .ics file onto the calendar surface previews the invitation before it is accepted. Shared calendars assigned by an administrator also appear automatically without a manual add step.

  • What this means for you: copy and paste of events was one of the loudest gaps for experienced calendar managers. If that is why you stayed on classic Outlook, it is worth another look.
  • Before you rely on it: Schedule View, multi-select, and conditional formatting are still outstanding - keep them on the scorecard.
UpdateRetiringMeetings

Meeting Insights is being retired

Message Center post MC1430531 retires Meeting Insights - the panel surfacing related emails and files inside meeting details - beginning mid-August 2026 and completing early September 2026. With a Copilot license you move to Copilot meeting preparation instead.

  • What this means for you: if you build pre-reads from Meeting Insights, move that routine over to Copilot meeting prep before the panel disappears.
  • Before you rely on it: affects Outlook web, New Outlook, mobile, Mac, and Teams. Without a Copilot license there is no direct replacement.
UpdateRetiringMobile

Send Availability is disappearing from Outlook mobile

Message Center post MC1393802 removed the Send Availability option from the Outlook mobile compose experience between mid-July and late July 2026. You now share availability by referencing the calendar manually instead.

  • What this means for you: a genuine step backwards if you coordinate times from your phone between meetings or while travelling.
  • Before you rely on it: this is a deliberate removal rather than a bug. Calendar functionality itself is unchanged.
Recently updatedShared mailbox

Shared mailbox archive is now available

The New Outlook comparison matrix lists shared mailbox archive as available, with a March 2026 status update.

  • What this means for you: better support when you need to look back through executive or team-managed mailboxes.
  • Before you rely on it: pair this with a search/archive validation exercise for high-volume executive offices.
Recently updatedDelegation

Shared mailbox delegate access moved, but is still partial

Shared mailbox delegate access is marked partially available with an April 2026 status update and roadmap tracking.

  • What this means for you: do not assume every shared mailbox or executive support pod workflow is fully covered yet.
  • Before you rely on it: test Send As, Send on Behalf, category use, and folder access against a shared mailbox you actually use.
Newer behaviorMeetings

Follow meetings changes the "I need awareness" pattern

Follow lets an attendee show the time as free while still signaling interest in recording, transcript, and follow-up.

  • What this means for you: useful when an executive should not attend but wants the assistant or organizer to preserve meeting artifacts.
  • Before you rely on it: organizers in classic Outlook may see Follow as tentative.
AvailableMeeting artifacts

Meeting recap is now a calendar workflow

New Outlook surfaces supported Teams meeting recap links directly from the calendar event, including files, transcript, and recording.

  • What this means for you: you can pull post-meeting material straight from the calendar instead of hunting across Teams chats and files.
  • Before you rely on it: access remains governed by each artifact's original permissions.
CopilotNewer flow

Schedule with Copilot turns email threads into meeting drafts

In New Outlook, Copilot can analyze an email thread, draft the meeting title and agenda, add thread participants, and attach the source thread.

  • What this means for you: reduces manual effort when converting long approval or planning threads into a decision meeting.
  • Before you rely on it: requires Microsoft 365 Copilot work licensing and sufficient thread context.
CopilotNewer flow

Meeting prep now surfaces context inside the event

Copilot can prepare you for a meeting by summarizing relevant context, tasks, documents, and resources from the meeting event form.

  • What this means for you: supports faster executive briefing prep when the assistant has access to the relevant meeting content.
  • Before you rely on it: tenant Meeting Insights settings and user permissions affect the experience.
Recently updatedMail files

.msg support and Quick Parts are listed as available

The matrix lists .msg support and Quick Parts as available with April 2026 status updates; .oft support remains partial.

  • What this means for you: helpful for saved executive messages, recurring language blocks, and reusable response content.
  • Before you rely on it: test any existing template-heavy workflows that depend on OFT files.
CopilotMay rollout

Copilot account selector reduces multi-account confusion

When more than one Copilot-enabled account is added to New Outlook, the Copilot side pane in Calendar includes an account dropdown.

  • What this means for you: lets you confirm which account Copilot is acting on when you hold more than one.
  • Before you rely on it: include this in validation for anyone juggling multiple work identities or delegated contexts.
UpdatedOffline

Offline support continues to improve

Microsoft notes support for adding attachments while offline, a 30-day offline mail sync window, offline search folders, and undo send while offline.

  • What this means for you: better resilience for travel, stores, facilities, and variable connectivity.
  • Before you rely on it: still test real travel/offline scenarios before removing classic Outlook fallback.
Coming / changedCalendar

Calendar power-user items are still moving

Schedule View, multi-select calendar events, copy/cut/paste events, conditional formatting, and teammate calendar surfacing are still listed as upcoming.

  • What this means for you: this is where experienced calendar managers may feel the most friction.
  • Before you rely on it: keep these items on the pilot scorecard, not buried in appendix material.
AvailableTriage

Modern triage patterns are now part of the pitch

Pin, snooze, sweep, schedule send, reactions, category favorites, and drag-and-drop file improvements are available in New Outlook.

  • What this means for you: these are the practical wins to demonstrate live, especially for high-volume executive inboxes.
  • Before you rely on it: show how these replace daily workarounds rather than explaining them as "new features."
RoadmapDelegation

Mail delegate permission management is on the roadmap

Roadmap item 417063 says you will be able to grant mailbox and folder delegate access from within New Outlook, planned for July 2026.

  • What this means for you: should make setting up delegate access with your executive and IT simpler.
  • Before you rely on it: until available, keep current Exchange/Outlook delegate setup guidance in the rollout plan.
Why this matters now: you already know where the old gaps were. The items above are the ones that have actually moved, and they are what should change your view of New Outlook.

Migration roadmap: opt-in today, opt-out and cutover later with notice.

Microsoft describes three progressive stages for New Outlook migration: opt-in, opt-out, and cutover. Managed Enterprise plans receive at least 12 months notice before opt-out and cutover stages in production rings.

03 / ROADMAP

Stage 1: Opt-in

You can try New Outlook with the toggle, switch back if you need to, and in some cases run classic and New Outlook side by side to compare.

General availability

New Outlook became generally available in August 2024, with feature development continuing after GA.

Stage 2: Opt-out

New Outlook becomes on by default after notice; you can still revert to classic Outlook while the opt-out stage applies.

Stage 3: Cutover

You can no longer switch back. Existing classic Outlook installations remain supported until at least 2029.

EA impact Adoption planning

What this means for you

  • No surprise cliff: you will get advance notice well before any stage change that removes your ability to switch back.
  • Try it with guardrails: you can move to New Outlook while keeping classic Outlook available for the edge workflows you cannot risk.
  • Your feedback counts: use Help > Give Feedback in New Outlook to influence what Microsoft builds next.
  • Plan around your own complexity: if you run high-volume delegation, shared mailboxes, custom forms, macros, or COM add-ins, test those specifically.
Worth knowing: readiness depends on your workflows, not your job title. A colleague with a simpler mailbox may be ready long before you are, because you manage multiple executives and shared resources.

Where New Outlook helps your day-to-day execution.

The changes that matter most to you are in calendar delegation, meeting response handling, meeting recap, mailbox organization, and rapid task capture.

04 / EA WORKFLOWS
AvailableCalendar

Delegate calendar access

New Outlook supports editor and delegate calendar access for work or school accounts in Microsoft 365 / Exchange Online.

  • Delegates can schedule and respond to meetings on behalf of the executive.
  • Owners can allow private-event visibility and category management.
  • Meeting request routing can be delegate only, delegate plus copy, or both owner and delegate.
AvailableMeetings

Follow a meeting

Attendees who cannot join can choose Follow, signaling they want recordings, transcripts, and follow-up while showing the time as free.

  • Useful when your executive wants awareness without blocking their calendar.
  • Organizers receive a visible Follow response and are reminded to record.
  • Classic Outlook organizers may see the response as tentative.
AvailableTeams + Outlook

Meeting recap in calendar

Past Teams meetings can show links to files, transcript, and recording directly from the Outlook calendar event.

  • Find meeting outcomes without digging through Teams.
  • Does not store content in Outlook; it links to original locations.
  • Each meeting asset retains its own access permissions.
AvailableProductivity

My Day with Calendar and To Do

My Day keeps upcoming events and tasks visible from Mail, Calendar, and People, including quick task and event creation.

  • Drag an email into To Do to create a task.
  • Join Teams meetings without switching to full calendar view.
  • Show additional calendars in the My Day pane.
AvailableMail

Modern message management

New Outlook includes pin, snooze, sweep, schedule send, reactions, and category favorites to speed up the way you triage an executive mailbox.

  • Pin high-priority asks while processing mail.
  • Snooze messages until a decision window.
  • Use Sweep for recurring low-value mail.
ValidateShared mailbox

Shared mailbox improvements

Shared mailboxes and shared folders are available. Shared mailbox archive is listed as available as of March 2026, while shared mailbox delegate access is partially available as of April 2026.

  • Test your executive and team shared mailbox scenarios before you switch your main machine over.
  • Confirm Send As and Send on Behalf behave the way you expect, and raise it with IT if not.

Copilot in New Outlook: your biggest productivity accelerators.

Copilot features depend on licensing and permissions. The value is strongest when you already have access to the mailbox, thread, calendar event, file, transcript, or meeting content.

05 / COPILOT
UpdateCopilotNotebooks

Pull Outlook emails into Copilot Notebooks

Message Center post MC1392569 (Roadmap 564910) lets Outlook emails be added as references in Copilot Notebooks alongside files and links, with GA rolling out mid-July through late July 2026.

  • How you would use it: build a durable notebook per initiative that includes the actual email decisions, then generate briefs, summaries, and decks grounded in that thread history.
  • Watchout: notebook references inherit source permissions - confirm what a shared notebook exposes before circulating it.
UpdateCopilotClassic Outlook

Copilot settings reach classic Outlook for Windows

Message Center post MC1358831 (Roadmap 561491) adds Copilot settings to the File menu in classic Outlook for Windows, including the option to turn Copilot on or off. GA ran early July to late July 2026.

  • How you would use it: useful for the population still on classic Outlook while the migration runs - settings now sit where the feature is used.
  • Good to know: this does not change Copilot licensing, eligibility, or existing admin controls.
CopilotAvailable

Summarize email threads and attachments

Copilot can summarize email conversations and cite source messages. In New Outlook you can also summarize supported Word, PowerPoint, and PDF attachments straight from the email.

  • How you would use it: create a quick brief from long scheduling, travel, or approval threads.
  • Watchout: Copilot only summarizes content the user can access.
CopilotAvailable

Schedule with Copilot from an email thread

New Outlook can analyze a thread, create a meeting invitation, add the people on the thread, draft a title and agenda, and attach the email thread.

  • How you would use it: convert a messy back-and-forth into a structured meeting draft.
  • Watchout: confidential or low-context discussions may not generate agenda content.
CopilotAvailable

Prepare for your meeting

Copilot can surface meeting context, related tasks, documents, and insights in Outlook meeting details, with deeper chat prompts available.

  • How you would use it: assemble pre-reads or last-minute context for executives.
  • Good to know: Microsoft documents a Meeting Insights tenant setting in the Microsoft 365 admin center.
CopilotAvailable

Draft and coach email

Copilot can draft messages, summarize, coach tone, and help refine executive communications inside Outlook.

  • How you would use it: move from rough executive intent to polished draft faster.
  • Permissions check: verify appropriate delegation and mailbox access before relying on assistant-driven Copilot flows.
CopilotNewer flow

Add Outlook email context into Copilot Chat

Implicit Grounding in Outlook lets you add selected emails or thread text directly into Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat prompts.

  • How you would use it: ground escalation briefs, meeting prep, and draft replies in the exact email context being reviewed.
  • Watchout: still verify the selected source context and citations before sending executive communications.
CopilotMulti-account

Confirm the active Copilot account

The account selector in the Outlook Copilot side pane lets you switch between Copilot-enabled accounts without relying on Outlook's broader account context.

  • How you would use it: reduce mistakes when switching between personal work, project, and delegated executive contexts.
  • Good to know: if you hold more than one account, check the selector before you trust a Copilot answer.

Watchouts: the classic Outlook workflows most likely to affect you.

These are the areas to check before you make New Outlook your default.

07 / WATCHOUTS
Not supported

COM add-ins, VBA, MAPI/OOM, custom forms

New Outlook uses a modern web add-in model and does not support several classic Outlook extension patterns.

  • List the add-ins or macros you rely on daily.
  • Check whether the vendor offers a web add-in version.
Partial

Rules, Quick Steps, offline, PST

Several classic productivity and resilience features are partially available rather than fully equivalent.

  • Write down the automation you use most.
  • Confirm offline behavior when you travel or work from a store or facility with patchy connectivity.
Upcoming / investigating

Calendar power-user features

Schedule View, multi-select calendar events, copy/cut/paste calendar events, and conditional formatting are marked upcoming; custom holidays are under investigation.

  • Note how you work complex calendars today.
  • Keep classic Outlook available if these gaps block critical scheduling.
Not supported

Network shares and SharePoint calendar sync

Accessing files on network shares and classic SharePoint calendar sync are listed as not supported.

  • Move repeat workflows to cloud file links or group calendars where you can.
Not supported

Voting buttons

Voting buttons are listed as not supported in New Outlook.

  • Use Forms or an approval flow when you need to poll a group.
Partial

Public folders and search folders

Both are partially available, which may matter for legacy team processes.

  • Check whether your executive's office uses public folders for shared reference or routing.
