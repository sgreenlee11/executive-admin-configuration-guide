Run your own day faster with Copilot

Before you ever act on the executive's behalf, Copilot in Outlook and Copilot Chat accelerate your day. Open Copilot inline in Outlook to summarize a thread or draft a reply, or open Copilot Chat (in Teams, the M365 app, or chat.microsoft.com) to catch up across email + Teams + files in a single prompt. Every action below works on items you own — no delegate setup needed.

🎛️ Filter ideas

Filters you can combine in any calendar instruction:

  • Subject — keyword or exact match

  • Duration — short (≤30 min), long (≥60 min)
  • Attendee count — small (≤5), large (≥10), 1:1 (exactly 2)
  • Cancellation status
  • Calendar conflicts
